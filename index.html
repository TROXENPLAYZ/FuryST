The vision

Built for exploration.
Designed to grow.

Elyndor aims to combine the freedom of a voxel world with the progression of a fantasy RPG. These are the pillars guiding development—not a claim that every feature is already implemented.

A changing world

Procedurally generated voxel terrain and streamed chunks designed to support a vast world.

Fantasy progression

Long-term plans for combat, enemies, abilities, elemental powers and RPG progression.
